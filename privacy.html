Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy covers Tech-Prank Publisher ("the app"), a private tool operated by the Tech-Prank channel owner to upload the channel's own videos to the channel's own YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok accounts.

Who uses the app

Only the Tech-Prank channel owner. The app is not offered to the public, and no other users can sign in.

What data the app accesses

  • Basic profile information of the channel's own accounts (for example account ID and display name), used only to confirm the correct account is connected.
  • Access/refresh tokens granted by the platforms, used only to upload the channel's own videos.

What the app does not do

  • It does not access other people's accounts, followers' data, messages or contacts.
  • It does not sell, share or use data for advertising.
  • It does not use cookies or analytics.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in a private GitHub repository and on the owner's computer, and are never published. They can be revoked at any time from each platform's account settings.

Deleting data

Revoking the app's access in the platform's settings stops all access; the owner then deletes the stored tokens.

Contact

Message the Tech-Prank accounts (Instagram @techprank7).
